A little about us

Your wedding morning, made special.

I’m Jess, the makeup artist and beautician behind The Loft Beauty Studio. I love helping each bride feel comfortable, confident, and beautifully herself. A bit about me: I am a mum of two, navigating the chaos of motherhood whilst running my beauty business. I've worked in the beauty industry for 15 years and can't imagine doing anything else. My favourite thing about my career is making my gorgeous clients feel good about themselves. I love all of the treatments I do, but my favourites have to be bridal makeup and brows! I am obsessed with live music. Concerts and festivals are my happy place. I have a passion for cooking and baking at home, but also love the gym as a little escape for some "me time". It's all about balance; give me a cappuccino in the morning, an iced matcha in the afternoon, and a mojito when the sun goes down. I've been lucky enough to explore some amazing countries, but Italy has my heart <3
